Appraised values (primary)

Douglas County Appraiser's Office — 2026 values, file DgCo2026Values.xlsx.

  • Received 2026-10-30 in response to a free Kansas Open Records Act (KORA) request.
  • Answered by County Appraiser Brad Eldridge (no fee).
  • Columns: PropertyNumber, RP_AprTot, RP_AsdTotal, fpropclass, TaxUnitGroupCode, TaxUnitGroupDesc, vproplevel_TotalAcres.
  • No owner names were requested or included.
