Questions

Is this the same as my tax bill?

No. The map shows appraised value per acre from the county Appraiser, not the tax you pay. Assessed value and mill levies are separate.

Why is $/acre so high for ordinary houses?

Total appraised value includes the building. Dividing by lot acres makes small lots look “taller” per acre than large lots with the same house value — that comparison is intentional.

Do you show who owns each parcel?

No. Owner names were not requested under KORA and are not in the published GeoJSON.

Why don't the tallest towers match a linear dollar scale?

Height and color use a continuous log scale so a few giant parcels don't flatten everyone else into a plateau. Popups still show true appraised dollars and $/acre.

Can I get the data?

Parcel geometry and zoning come from public GIS services. The 2026 value table was released by the Douglas County Appraiser under KORA; see Data.

Can I hide government buildings?

You can hide tax-exempt parcels (class E): government, schools, churches, and other exempt uses. The county file has no government-only flag, so we cannot isolate government alone. The toggle on the map and Findings page stay in sync.

Is this an official city or county product?

No. It is an independent civic map. Not affiliated with the City of Lawrence, Douglas County, Urban3, Pracre, or Strong Towns.

How do I rotate the map on a desktop?

Right-drag (or Ctrl/Shift+left-drag), press Q/E or Shift+arrow keys, or use the rotate buttons. The compass resets north.
